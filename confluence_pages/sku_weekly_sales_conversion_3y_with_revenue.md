**Weekly Revenue Performance Report**

Three-Year Weekly Sales Analysis — January 2023 to December 2025

# **Executive Summary**

This report presents a comprehensive three-year review of weekly revenue performance across four key product lines within the portfolio. The data spans the period from January 2023 through December 2025, encompassing 156 weeks of sales activity for each product. The four products covered in this report are the NovaPhone X1 (SKU: NPX1), the NovaHome SmartHub Pro (SKU: NSH-PRO), the NovaFit Active Watch (SKU: NFA-WATCH), and the NovaCart Essentials Subscription (SKU: NC-ESS). Each weekly record captures units sold, total site visits, conversion rate, unit price in USD, and resulting weekly revenue in USD. All data presented herein is sourced directly from the company's internal sales and web analytics systems and reflects actuals with no adjustments or projections.

The report is organized into four dedicated product sections, each providing context on the product's positioning within the portfolio followed by a complete weekly data table. This structure allows readers to quickly reference any specific week's performance for a given product, or to review trends across the full reporting window.

# **Product Overview**

The portfolio analyzed in this report consists of four distinct product lines, each serving a different market segment. The NovaPhone X1 is the company's premium flagship smartphone, consistently generating the highest weekly revenue figures due to its elevated unit price. The NovaHome SmartHub Pro serves the growing smart home category, offering connectivity features at a mid-range price point. The NovaFit Active Watch targets health-conscious consumers in the wearables market, occupying a mid-premium position. Finally, the NovaCart Essentials Subscription rounds out the portfolio as a high-volume, low-price digital subscription offering, which contributes meaningfully to customer lifetime value and recurring revenue streams.

The reporting period of January 2023 to December 2025 captures three full calendar years of operations, providing a sufficient window to observe seasonal patterns, pricing dynamics, and volume trends across all four product lines. Key metrics tracked throughout the report include weekly units sold, site traffic (expressed as site visits), the resulting conversion rate, the prevailing unit price in US dollars, and total weekly revenue in US dollars.
